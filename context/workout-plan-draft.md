# Training plan v2 — tightened (2026-09-19)

Waiting approval. Audit: v1 too long on a cut; bench missing from B breaks 2-day weeks; 5 pull-up sets too much next to rows.

## Constraints

- 91 kg, cut ~3 kg/month. Training keeps muscle. Diet makes the cut.
- Goals: first overhand pull-up (near); abs; cardio; bench 100 kg later (almost no barbell bench yet).
- 3–4 gym days, work skips. Calendar slots + queue shift.
- Gym + home (push-ups / plank / abs on non-gym days).
- Each gym starts with 15 min stair-climber 6–7 (treadmill swap OK).
- Squat already 80×5.

## Structure

Queue **A → B → C → A**. Target slots e.g. Mon/Wed/Fri + optional 4th. Miss → same letter moves to next gym day. 4th day = next letter.

Effort: ~2–3 reps in reserve. No failure. No extra HIIT.

Bench **every gym session** (heavy / light / volume) so 2-day weeks still hit bench twice.

## Gym — every session (~55–70 min)

1. Stair 15 min
2. Four lifts. Bench first.
3. Done.

### Day A — heavy bench + squat

- Bench 3×5–8. Start: 8 clean with 2–3 RIR. All sets hit 8 → +2.5 kg next A.
- Squat 3×5 from 80 kg. All 3×5 easy → +2.5 kg.
- Pull-ups 3–4 sets (ladder)
- Row 3×8–12

### Day B — light bench + hinge + press

- Bench 3×5, ~10% lighter than A, extra 1 RIR, pause on chest OK
- Overhead press 3×5–8, same double progression as bench
- Romanian deadlift 3×8
- Pull-ups 3–4 sets

### Day C — volume bench + legs

- Bench 3×8–10, ~10% lighter than A
- Leg press or walking lunge 3×8–12 (not a second heavy squat; stair already taxed legs)
- Pull-ups 3–4 sets
- Lat pulldown 3×10–12

## Pull-up ladder — every gym, 3–4 sets, not to failure

1. No reps yet: 5s negatives × 3, or band
2. 1–4 ugly: 3–4 × 1–3, leave 1–2 in reserve
3. 3–4 × 5 clean: add a slow negative after last set
4. That easy: +2.5–5 kg belt, back to 3×3

No home bar assumed.

## Home — days without gym

- Plank 2–3×, from 60s, +5–10s when all holds easy. ~120s then harder variation.
- Push-ups 3 sets, 2–3 RIR. «30» is a ceiling.
- Abs 2×10–15

Gym day: skip home push-ups. Optional 1 plank.

## Deload

Sleep/work wrecks a week, or 6–8 weeks in: same days, half sets or −10% load.

## Drop

- Hourly nags
- HIIT as required work
- Pool as required cardio
- 5–6 lifts after the stair
- Failure on daily 30 push-ups

## Honest timeline

- Pull-up: weeks to a few months. Cut helps.
- Abs look: mostly the cut + this direct work.
- Cardio: stair 15 is the dose. Don’t add.
- Bench 100: not this cut-block. First months: learn the lift, add 2.5 kg when reps are there. 100 kg after the easy neural stretch, often when the cut slows.
